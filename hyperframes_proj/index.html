WHAT DOES A DERIVATIVE ACTUALLY MEASURE?
ZOOM INTO ANY CURVE, CLOSE ENOUGH, AND IT
LOOKS ALMOST STRAIGHT.
THAT STRAIGHT LINE'S SLOPE IS THE DERIVATIVE.
WATCH THIS SECOND POINT SLIDE IN.
THE SECANT LINE SWINGS CLOSER...
AND CLOSER...
UNTIL IT LOCKS ONTO THE EXACT TANGENT.
THAT SLOPE IS THE RATE OF CHANGE, AT THIS EXACT
INSTANT.
NOT AN AVERAGE.
NOT AN ESTIMATE.
THE INSTANT ITSELF.
